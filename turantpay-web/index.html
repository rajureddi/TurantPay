bolt About TurantPay

TurantPay is an offline-first digital payments web application designed for India. It bridges smart devices with NPCI's *99# USSD platform, allowing you to send money, scan QR codes, and check bank balance without an active internet connection.

cell_tower About *99# USSD Service

*99# is NPCI’s National Unified USSD Platform (NUUP). It brings banking services across all telecom service providers in India over basic cellular signals. Transactions are processed directly by your registered bank using GSM network channels.
